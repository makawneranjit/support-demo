# Nimbus Sync help center (fictional demo product)

## Reset your password
1. On the sign-in page, choose "Forgot password".
2. Enter your account email.
3. Open the reset link within 30 minutes.
4. Choose a new password of at least 12 characters.
If no email arrives within 10 minutes, check spam, then contact support.

## Sync stuck or error SYNC-409
SYNC-409 means your sign-in token expired while a sync was running.
Fix: sign out of the desktop app, sign back in, then click "Resume sync".
If it stalls again, go to Settings > Advanced > Clear cache and retry.
Folders with more than 50,000 files can take up to an hour to resume.

## Storage limits
The Free plan includes 5 GB and the Pro plan includes 200 GB.
When storage is full, sync pauses with error SYNC-507.
Delete files or upgrade your plan to continue.

## Billing and refunds
Support agents cannot change charges or issue refunds.
All billing and refund requests go to the billing team,
who respond within 2 business days.
